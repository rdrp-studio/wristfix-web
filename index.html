How it works

From your boat's network to your wrist, in four steps.

The phone does all the work, so the watch battery lasts. Nothing is installed on the boat: WRISTFIX uses the WiFi gateway you already have, or any standard one.

01

Your instruments

Chartplotter, depth, wind, AIS, autopilot and engine share data on NMEA 2000 or NMEA 0183.

02

A WiFi gateway

A WiFi bridge or a SignalK server puts that data on the boat's WiFi.

03

The phone app

Finds the gateway by itself, decodes the data and runs the alarms, even with the screen off.

04

Your watch

Eleven pages at a glance, a tile and complications, updated every second.

On the watch

Everything you check at the helm, one swipe away.

Swipe, turn the bezel or tap to move between pages. Each kind of value keeps its own colour on every page, and red always means an alarm.

Safety

Man overboard, with one long press

Hold the red button and WRISTFIX saves the position, then guides you back with distance, bearing and an arrow. It can also send the MOB to your chartplotters over NMEA 2000, NMEA 0183 or SignalK.

Instruments

Depth in large figures, speed over ground and through water, course and heading. Shallow water turns it red.

Plotter with AIS

Your boat, track and course on a chart, with every AIS ship around you and a collision warning based on CPA.

Tap a ship

Name, type, MMSI, call sign, course, speed, CPA, destination and size, just like on a chartplotter.

Sleep well

Anchor alarm on your wrist and your phone

Drop the anchor with a long press and choose the swing radius. If the boat drags or the GPS is lost, the watch vibrates and the phone sounds an alarm, every minute until you act.

Wind

Apparent and true wind on a dial with close-hauled sectors, plus the strongest gust of the last ten minutes.

Weather

Barometric pressure with a 3-hour trend and barograph, and a warning when it falls fast.

Waypoint

Distance, bearing, VMG and ETA to the next waypoint of your route, with the cross-track error.

Autopilot control

Engage, standby, ±1° and ±10°, and tack, for Raymarine Evolution, Simrad, B&G and SignalK pilots. Optional.

Engine

RPM, coolant temperature, oil pressure, alternator, fuel rate and engine hours, with alarms.

Tanks

Fuel, water and holding tanks with your own names and the litres or gallons left.

Batteries

Voltage and charge of each battery bank, named your way, with a low-battery alarm.

!

Designed as a navigation aid, with safety first

  • The MOB function never sends any command to the autopilot. Steering back to the person in the water is always the skipper's decision.
  • Sending data to the boat and autopilot control are off by default, and you confirm a warning before enabling them.
  • Every page shows when its data is missing or stale.
  • WRISTFIX does not replace the vessel's own instruments, a proper lookout or good seamanship.

The phone app connects to the boat, runs the alarms in the background and keeps your watch up to date.

FAQ

Questions sailors ask.

Does it work with an iPhone or an Apple Watch?

No. WRISTFIX needs an Android phone and a Wear OS watch paired with it.

Does the watch work without the phone?

No. The phone connects to the boat and sends the data to the watch, so it must stay on board within Bluetooth range.

Do I need to change anything on my boat?

Only if you have no WiFi gateway. Any of the gateways in the compatibility table, or a SignalK server, is enough.

Will I lose internet on my phone?

No. If the gateway's WiFi has no internet, Android keeps using mobile data for everything else, and WRISTFIX downloads charts over it. With a router on board, the phone has internet over WiFi.

Can it steer my boat?

Only if you enable autopilot control and confirm the warning. It sends the same commands as your pilot's own control head. The man overboard function never sends commands to the autopilot.

Is the chart suitable for navigation?

No. The watch chart is drawn from OpenStreetMap, OpenFreeMap and OpenSeaMap data to help you see where you are. It is not an official nautical chart.

What data does WRISTFIX collect?

None. Your boat's data stays on your phone and watch. Read the privacy policy.
